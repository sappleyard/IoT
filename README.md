|---|---|
| [Aircraft-Noise/](Aircraft-Noise/) | Measures how loud aircraft are at home: two ESP32 microphone nodes and a Raspberry Pi with an ADS-B receiver, the analysis and the aircraft classifier trained on the data, and an announcer that announces unusual aircraft the mics hear on the Sonos, and a public site of heard flights with a 10-day pass forecast. No audio is ever recorded. |
