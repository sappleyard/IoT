About this log

Two microphones at the house measure sound levels (never recording audio), and a small receiver picks up the position broadcasts aircraft send. A flight is heard when a model trained on those levels hears an aircraft and the broadcasts show which one it was. A pass is one aircraft coming within 5 km, measured in a straight line that includes its height, heard or not. Before 3 November the microphone levels were collected to train that model (training days), so those days have passes but no heard flights. Airline, route and aircraft details come from VRS standing data and adsbdb.
