Methodology

Detection: two microphones log sound levels once per second; no audio is recorded. A classifier trained on these levels flags aircraft noise, and each detection is matched to an aircraft using ADS-B position data from a local receiver.

Passes: a pass is any aircraft within 5 km slant range (horizontal distance and altitude combined), whether heard or not. Days before 3 November were used to collect training data, so they have passes but no detections.

Sources: airline, route and aircraft details from VRS standing data and adsbdb.
